# Firstpin — project brief

## What this is
Firstpin (firstpinlocal.com) is a Google Business Profile management service
for construction and home service businesses: contractors and local trades in
general, not one specific trade. We get them into the top 3 on Google Maps,
where the calls are.

## Offer
- $300/month, month-to-month. No setup fee for founding clients (first 3–5).
- Guarantee: if their Maps ranking hasn't improved after 30 days, month two is free.
- Lead magnet: a free personalised audit (their profile vs top 3 competitors).

## Services
- Setup (first 2 weeks): audit vs top 3 competitors, fix categories, complete all
  profile fields, upload job photos, fix directory listings, remove duplicates,
  set up a review request link/QR code.
- Monthly: weekly Google posts, weekly photos, reply to all reviews within 48h,
  review-generation texts/emails, answer profile Q&A, monitor for fake reviews and
  suspension risks, track competitors.
- Reporting: one-page monthly report (views, calls, directions, clicks), map-ranking
  grid, plain-English summary.

## Audience
Owners of US construction and home service businesses (contractors, local
trades), often reading on a phone between jobs. Busy, skeptical of marketers,
get spammed by SEO agencies constantly.

Site copy stays trade-neutral: say "local service businesses", "construction and
home service businesses", "contractors", "your business", "top local competitor".
Don't name a single trade (no "roofers", "plumbers" etc.) as if it's the only one.

## Voice
Direct, short sentences, numbers first, contractor language. Say "jobs" and "calls".
Never say "leads", "conversions", "leverage", "SEO strategy" or "digital presence".

## Brand presentation
- Always "we". Never name an individual, never claim a team size, never invent
  staff names. The site must not read as a one-person business.
- No testimonials section until real client feedback exists. No fake quotes.
- Footer: "© 2026 Firstpin, a Humanaira Ltd company. Registered in England &
  Wales." plus Humanaira Ltd's registered office address (from Companies House).

## Design: bold industrial
Job-site signage meets newspaper front page.
- Navy #1B2A41 (headings, logo), safety orange #F26B1D (buttons, key numbers),
  off-white #F7F5F0 (background), charcoal #2B2B2B (body), green #2E9E5B (only
  for "up" metrics).
- Archivo Black for headlines (huge, tight), Inter for body.
- Square corners, thick dividers, high contrast. Mobile-first.
- NEVER: gradients, glassmorphism, rows of 3 icon cards, stock photos,
  "AI-powered" badges, sparkle icons. It must not look AI-generated.

## Tech
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind. Deploy on Vercel.
